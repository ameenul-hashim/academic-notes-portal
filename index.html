The New Learning Platform

BA History BA Sociology

Calicut Unofficial Notes has now been rebranded as NoteSphere. To provide a modern, reliable, and long-term learning experience, we have completely redesigned the application with a new interface, improved features, and a better system architecture. NoteSphere is built to support both current and future students, ensuring continued access to study materials for upcoming semesters and future academic years.

Who Can Use NoteSphere?

NoteSphere is designed for BA History and BA Sociology students. The platform provides semester-wise study materials in both English and Malayalam. Each semester contains organized subjects, notes, and learning resources that can be viewed or downloaded directly within the application.

Create Your NoteSphere Account

To access the study materials, every student must create a NoteSphere account.

Registration Requirements

  • Full Name – Enter your official full name.
  • Username – Choose a unique username or nickname that you can easily remember.
  • Email Address – Required for account verification, password recovery, and security notifications.
  • Phone Number – Optional.
  • Password – Must meet the following security requirements:
    • At least 8 characters long
    • At least one uppercase letter (A–Z)
    • At least one lowercase letter (a–z)

If you ever forget your password, password reset instructions and OTP verification will be sent to your registered email address.

Login

After creating your account, simply log in using your Username and Password. Once logged in, you can access all available semester-wise study materials according to your course.

Learning Features

Inside NoteSphere you can:

  • Browse semester-wise subjects.
  • Read notes directly within the application.
  • Download study materials for offline learning.
  • Access content in both English and Malayalam.
  • Continue using the same account throughout your academic journey.

Long-Term Availability

Your NoteSphere account is designed for long-term use. As new semesters and study materials are added, your existing account will continue to provide access without requiring a new registration.

Community Guidelines

Please use NoteSphere responsibly. The following activities are strictly prohibited:

  • Unauthorized copying or redistribution of study materials.
  • Copyright infringement.
  • Attempting to exploit, modify, or misuse the application.
  • Any activity that violates the application's Terms of Use.

Any user found engaging in copyright violations, unauthorized distribution of content, or misuse of the platform may have their account permanently banned, resulting in the loss of access to NoteSphere and all associated services.
